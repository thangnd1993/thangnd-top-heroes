The VIP, Recruit, and Home-navigation anchors in this folder were cropped from verified
`2 / 5-Emmmmm` survey captures on 2026-09-20 UTC. They identify the page, explicit
free wording, independently visible availability, the intended action, and Back.
The full captures and account/boot sidecars remain outside Git under the Manager's
local diagnostics directory.

Every runtime target must be matched uniquely in a **fresh screenshot of the
current account**. Crop coordinates are provenance, not tap coordinates or an
authority for another account. The Recruit action is the left free button; the
ticket-priced x10 button is excluded. The VIP paid package is excluded.

The Home-navigation crops matched two Home survey frames; Tavern selection crops
matched two selected-building frames and did not match a plain Home frame. They
still require a fresh same-account screenshot and destination verification after
each individual navigation action.

These fixtures do **not** contain a verified post-claim anchor. Until that is
collected and regression-tested, a live claim must
fail closed; a red dot or a precondition match alone is not acceptance evidence.
The `Tiệm` gift has no independent free-cost proof in the index 2 survey and has
no claim anchor here.
