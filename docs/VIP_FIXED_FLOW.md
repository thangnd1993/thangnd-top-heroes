# Fixed VIP flow

The annotated upper gift and green daily Nhận are known free targets. Detect
them on the current VIP screenshot, exclude the paid/VND region, and click each
active target once. Skip inactive targets. UNKNOWN receives no input.

- Upper gift: known icon plus adjacent qualified attention badge; observe at
  most three frames, 0.5 seconds apart, when an animation phase is inconclusive.
- Inactive gift: unique positive badge-free icon and clear attention area.
- Daily: enabled free Nhận; inactive daily: disabled/countdown button.
- After one claim, capture immediately. Dismiss a qualified receipt or promo
  once at the user-approved bottom-left point, then capture again.
- Success is AVAILABLE -> one tap -> NOT_AVAILABLE. A popup is not required
  and is not proof by itself. Unknown outcomes retain their duplicate lock.
